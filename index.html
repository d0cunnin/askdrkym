01  About

A leading voice in integrated health & wellness.

Dr. Kym Leonard is a Board-Certified Doctor of Nursing Practice and Certified Master Counselor, with specialized expertise in Mental and Behavioral Health and Addiction Medicine.

She brings a unique, multidimensional perspective to care, bridging clinical excellence with compassion and practical insight. In addition to her thriving private practice, Dr. Kym collaborates with local, national, and international organizations to advance mental health and addiction awareness, particularly within the scope of primary care integration.

Her work is recognized in academic and professional spaces alike. She is a published scholarly author in the Johns Hopkins Medical Journal, the creator of professional curricula, and the author of multiple therapy manuals used by practitioners and educators.

With both clinical depth and real-world compassion, Dr. Kym equips individuals, providers, and organizations to heal from the inside out.

04  Ministry

Global Kingdom Mandate.

Apostle Dr. Kym Leonard is the founder of G.K.M. – Global Kingdom Mandate, an international apostolic-prophetic training ministry and marketplace organization headquartered in Roswell, Georgia.

Her passion lies in training and equipping disciples. Recognized as a prophetic watchman and apostolic midwife, Apostle Dr. Kym is uniquely called to assist those in spiritual labor, encouraging them to “let go” and become vessels for birthing the ministries within them.

She actively leads and serves in both national and international missions.
